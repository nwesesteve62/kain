Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about rate structures or multi-carrier logistics? Find direct information below.

Our discounted commercial pricing rates are built directly using combined shipping metrics. By pooling carrier package volumes from over 100,000 retail shippers worldwide, we secure premier pre-negotiated tier rates and pass those pricing models to your VeloShip dashboard automatically.
No setup fees apply. Our core account setup tiers require no monthly pricing, enabling you to purchase carrier labels strictly as you pack. Additional dedicated professional tooling features are available on advanced billing plans should your operations expand.
We support popular ecommerce platforms including Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, Amazon, BigCommerce, and custom integrations built directly using our documentation APIs.
For international routes, customs declarations are formatted automatically. You can choose to prepay duties (DDP) or have the recipient settle duties upon cross-border delivery (DDU) during the label creation process.
